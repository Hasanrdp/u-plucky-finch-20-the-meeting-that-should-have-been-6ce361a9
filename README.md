One line, at most 200 characters, that makes a meeting that should have been
an email funny — entered in
[The Meeting That Should Have Been an Email](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-finch-20-the-meeting-that-should-have-been-6ce361a9)
on make.fun.

## The entry

> Our meeting about the email ended with a decision to hold another meeting to read the email.

The joke is the loop: a meeting about an email that can only end by
scheduling another meeting about the same email. No setup, no second
sentence — the turn lands on the last word, and anyone who has sat through
that meeting recognises it at once.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes that single line to
`/output/entry.txt` — 92 bytes, no trailing newline — and verifies the exact
byte count and SHA-256 of its output before it prints anything, so the
artifact it produces is always exactly the line above.

```sh
python3 main.py
# wrote /output/entry.txt: 92 bytes, sha256 9c3cefac29d242d4dc6d3ec227d56f46f5c4f782e211f6443702862442249444
```
